## Philosophy

Infrastructure should be:
- **Automated**: GitOps-driven, reproducible, version-controlled
- **Observable**: Comprehensive metrics, logs, and alerts
- **Secure**: Zero-trust architecture, minimal attack surface
- **Resilient**: High availability, disaster recovery, tested failure modes
- **Cost-effective**: Right-sized resources, efficient use of cloud services
